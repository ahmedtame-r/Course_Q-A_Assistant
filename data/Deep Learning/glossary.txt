Deep Learning Glossary

Epoch: One complete pass through the entire training dataset during model training.

Dropout: A regularization technique that randomly deactivates a fraction of neurons during training to reduce overfitting.

Batch Normalization: A technique that normalizes layer inputs to stabilize and accelerate training.

Vanishing Gradient: A problem in deep networks where gradients become extremely small during backpropagation, slowing or halting learning in early layers.

Transfer Learning: A technique where a model trained on one task is reused as the starting point for a model on a related task.
